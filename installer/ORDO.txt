Ordo - ratings for chess engines and players
============================================

ordo-win64.exe in this folder is Ordo 1.2.6, (C) 2015 Miguel A. Ballicora.
SPCT runs it (unmodified) to rate the game pairs of "spct gamepairs", exactly
as Stefan Pohl's Auto_Gamepairs_Rescorer_V1.6.bat does.
